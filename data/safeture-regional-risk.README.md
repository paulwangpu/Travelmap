# Safeture / Riskline Regional layer

Active Safeture basemap as of 2026-10-17 combines the original country overall assessment as its base with Regional advisories above it. A Regional assessment overrides the country assessment even when its category is lower. Within overlapping Regional advisories, the highest Regional category wins. Neither source geometry is replaced, clipped or merged.

Source: [Official interactive globe](https://www.safeture.com/globe/) and its [public Regional advisory dataset](https://globe.safeture.com/assets/geojson/riskline_advisory_simplified_description.geojson). The official globe's loader `G(..., 'regions')` filters `geomtype === 'regions'`; this implementation uses the identical filter. Of 153 source advisories, **110 Regional** records are retained and **43 country/multinational** records are excluded. No national overall rating or country-level advisory is expanded into regional geometry.

Every included source geometry, hole, multipolygon, original numeric category, title and update date is retained. The retained geographic/categorical metadata excludes descriptive article bodies and base64 flag graphics. Raw download, source hash and Last-Modified are recorded for provenance. This is a dated snapshot, not a real-time feed. Outside Regional coverage, the original country overall assessment is used. Areas without either assessment are grey (#d1d5db). The derived display mosaic reuses the existing Natural Earth polygon geometry from the Gallup asset as neutral land; its scores are never read for Safeture styling or assessments. This also keeps land absent from the 177-feature country snapshot grey.

Categories retain the official semantics: Negligible, Low, Medium, High, Critical. Chinese category wording is **极低 / 低 / 中 / 高 / 极高**, with **no numeric identifiers and no 风险 suffix**. English uses the corresponding words. The source's numeric level IDs remain internal data only. There are 2 Low, 27 Medium, 38 High and 43 Critical records in this snapshot; no Negligible record is present.

Local display colours are muted green, sage, sand, orange and rose: `#9fc9b7`, `#c7d8a5`, `#f2d695`, `#e9a174`, `#c96b78`. This replaces the source globe's olive/brown palette without changing category values. The existing opacity control applies once to an exclusive display mosaic, so overlapping assessments do not accumulate opacity or blend categories. MapLibre and Leaflet draw neutral land first, country overall assessments second, and Regional assessments last. Higher categories draw on top only within the Regional layer. Hover and click choose Regional first regardless of the country's category; country details are the fallback outside Regional coverage and never reuse a regional Summary. Labels whose representative point is covered by a higher category are suppressed, preventing a lower-category label on a higher-category fill. Country labels are additionally suppressed if their anchor falls inside any Regional polygon, including a lower category. Polygons themselves are never deleted or clipped for this label rule.

All risk fills, boundaries and labels remain below My coverage. Existing coverage fills, outlines, check-ins and historical layers are unchanged. The existing dropdown entry is named **Safeture 旅游风险 / Safeture Travel Risk**, before both Gallup entries and DEM; no new toggle or legend group is added. Safeture grade labels are not drawn on the map; categories remain in the legend and hover/details. Hover omits retrieval dates and the redundant Regional-priority sentence.

Rebuild: `python scripts/build-safeture-regional-risk.py` (standard library). Research/raw files stay under `output/safeture` and are not staged wholesale. Attribution: **Risk data and original geometry © Safeture / Riskline**; local display palette, no endorsement claimed.

Validation: `tests/safeture-risk.cjs` covers exact counts, original category distribution, geometry rings, exclusion of national advisories, word labels, overlapping category/label priority, cached switching, bilingual tooltips, coverage preservation and Leaflet ordering. Browser QA is `output/safeture/browser-regional.cjs` with world/Asia/Americas screenshots and a JSON result. Release remains 2.3.3; no commit or publication.

## Click details and Summary

Clicking a Regional polygon opens the existing right-side detail panel with its original advisory title, word category, affected areas, source update date, snapshot date and the public **Summary** excerpt. The excerpt stays in its original English and is labelled as such. An official source/attribution link remains visible. Multiple hit advisories are deduplicated and listed with the higher category first; previous/next controls preserve access to overlapping records.

`data/safeture-regional-summaries-20261017.json` contains 110 nonempty public Summary excerpts, each at most 155 English words. `scripts/build-safeture-regional-summaries.py` extracts these from the exact downloaded source matching the active geometry snapshot's SHA-256; it does not independently refetch a potentially different revision. Full report/advisory bodies and country descriptions are not embedded. The source HTML is converted to plain text with script/style content discarded, and all text is escaped again when displayed. No external HTML or flag image is inserted into the detail panel.

The Summary dictionary is fetched on the first stable hover (180ms) or click and cached for repeated hovers/selections. Hover shows a word-bounded preview of at most 280 characters, an explicit full-detail click hint and attribution. Brief flyovers do not fetch; asynchronous completion cannot reopen a departed tooltip. Clicking retains the complete original excerpt. Failures show retry feedback. Detail UI follows language changes while retaining the original English excerpt. Closing the panel or changing the basemap cleans up the selection; other point/details and check-in actions keep their existing priority. Adding points/drawing paths bypasses risk-detail selection. Leaflet country/admin background clicks delegate to the same Regional selection logic so the above-coverage interactive background cannot swallow the Summary action.

Additional verification: `tests/safeture-summary.cjs` tests snapshot matching, lazy cache, escaped text, overlap navigation, event/mode guards and cleanup. `output/safeture/browser-summary.cjs` uses physical clicks to verify original excerpts, dates, bilingual UI, close/reopen cache and basemap-switch cleanup; screenshots/results are stored alongside it.

Hover verification: `tests/safeture-hover-summary.cjs` tests delayed loading, rapid flyovers, a bounded escaped preview, cached reuse and suppression of late popup updates. `output/safeture/browser-hover-summary.cjs` verifies actual hover, click-through to the full excerpt, shared cache and leave/switch cleanup; screenshot `output/safeture/summary-hover-zh.png`.

## Uniform transparency

`data/safeture-display-20261017.geojson` is a derived render-only mosaic: six mutually exclusive category fills (including grey) and one deduplicated boundary network. Country and Regional snapshots remain unchanged. Regional categories are resolved first (highest Regional category in overlaps), then national overall assessments, then unassessed land. Natural Earth is used only as neutral land geometry; Gallup scores never contribute. Overlapping pieces of the same category are unioned as well.

`python scripts/build-safeture-display.py --deps <optional-shapely-directory>` rebuilds from existing local snapshots without network access. Shapely 2.x repairs invalid geometries and uses a 1e-7-degree grid (about 1 cm) solely for robust display overlay operations; these derived geometries do not increase source accuracy. Source hashes, display repairs, disjointness and independent priority samples are recorded. Original source geometry and holes remain available for assessment details.

MapLibre renders colour only from the display mosaic, with antialiasing disabled and zero simplification tolerance to keep adjacent category edges coincident. The original country/Regional fills remain at zero opacity for hover/click queries and overlapping-advisory navigation; they add no colour. Country/Regional outlines are zero-opacity too; a single deduplicated network renders visible boundaries. Leaflet uses the same mosaic for colour and transparent original features for interaction. No runtime polygon clipping is performed when changing zoom, provider or opacity. The derived data loads once per session and is included in the service-worker shell. My coverage retains its styles and stays above all risk rendering.

`tests/safeture-display.cjs` independently samples original precedence and checks that each assessed/land interior has exactly one display fill. `output/safeture/browser-opacity.cjs` verifies actual canvas pixels at 40% opacity against a single alpha blend for a lower Regional category over a higher national category and for unrated grey land. It also checks zero-opacity original hit detection, coverage preservation and provider cleanup. Screenshots/results stay under `output/safeture`.

## Clicked assessment boundary

Clicking an assessment draws its full original Polygon/MultiPolygon boundary, including holes, using a dark blue outline with a white halo and no fill. The Regional selection uses the original advisory geometry, not the disjoint display mosaic. National fallback resolves the full country source feature rather than the clipped rendered tile. The current overlap navigation selection controls the outline; mouse leave retains it. Closing/replacing the detail, switching provider or changing map engine clears it. MapLibre and Leaflet keep the selection outline in the Safeture rendering order below My coverage. Risk colours/opacity, check-ins and coverage styles remain unchanged.

`tests/safeture-selection.cjs` covers original geometry and holes, overlapping advisory navigation, country canonicalization, persistent click selection, late asynchronous completion after clearing, provider cleanup and Leaflet outlines. `output/safeture/browser-selection.cjs` verifies physical clicks, bilingual details, close/replaced-detail/provider cleanup, and saves selection screenshots.

## Country/territory counts

The existing legend shows counts from overall national assessments only, independently of Regional coverage or selected advisories. Records are deduplicated by valid ISO3, then ISO2, then source name. Current dated snapshot: Negligible 19, Low 45, Medium 65, High 26, Critical 12, no data 10; 177 source countries/territories in total, 167 rated. This counts the source coverage and does not claim a global sovereign-country total. Unmapped neutral land is not added as invented assessment records. Chinese/English legend checks and screenshot: `output/safeture/browser-counts.cjs`, `country-counts-zh.png`.
