# Unified country borders — China POV, 1:50 million

Natural Earth v5.1.2 repository. Uses ne_50m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land, ne_50m_admin_0_boundary_lines_disputed_areas and ne_50m_coastline.

Country and disputed lines use FCLASS_CN where populated, otherwise FEATURECLA. International boundary and disputed classes are displayed; Unrecognized, Admin-1 and Map unit boundaries are excluded. Source coordinates are preserved. Coastlines retain the previous coastal-outline behavior.

Sources: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/v5.1.2/geojson
Policy: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-cultural-vectors/50m-admin-0-boundary-lines-2/

This is Natural Earth's Chinese viewpoint, not an official Chinese standard map. Public domain. Loaded independently of visited areas and ethnic records.
